A life and business coach moved his business to [hopper.so](https://hopper.so) and saved ~$5,000 a year.

Daniel ([@dannybunny.co](https://www.instagram.com/dannybunny.co)) has a decade in the game and $1.7M in revenue across his practice. But behind the scenes, he was playing part-time IT manager.

He built LITO Academy on WordPress. He stitched together Kajabi, Kinsta, LearnDash, MemberPress, BuddyBoss, and Vimeo. He even paid a guy named Neel $500/year just to keep the lights on and debug webhooks.

Total cost: ~$6,130/year just for software.

Recently, he shut down LITO Academy and migrated his entire community to Hopper. (Full disclosure: Daniel is one of Hopper's backers, so he knows this space intimately.)

Same coach. Same members. Same content. New cost: $58/month.

That's an 89% savings. But the savings aren't the craziest part. It was going through his old receipts:

- $2,739 to BuddyBoss for an app that never shipped
- Kajabi raising rates from $199 to $249/mo with no warning
- Separate subscriptions just to host video and connect tools

Multiple dashboards, logins, and places for members to get lost. This is what most coaches are doing right now. Stitching. Patching. Paying. The platforms force them to do it. Daniel built a $1.7M practice in spite of his tech stack, not because of it.

That's who Hopper is for. One platform. $58/month. No vaporware apps. No webhook debugging. No retention pricing tricks. The Circle is live, and Daniel's whole community moved with him.

This is the work I get up for.
